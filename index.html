FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How often should I clean my windows?

    We recommend cleaning your windows about 4 times per year to keep them looking their best and help prevent long term buildup, including hard water etching.

  • Who is NestWise Services?

    NestWise Services is a family owned and operated local company providing residential and commercial window cleaning and exterior services throughout Utah, Salt Lake, Wasatch, and Summit County areas.

  • Do you offer any guarantee?

    Yes. We offer a 100% satisfaction guarantee before you pay.

    We also offer a 10 day weather guarantee with maintenance plans.

When you choose NestWise Services, you are supporting a local family.
